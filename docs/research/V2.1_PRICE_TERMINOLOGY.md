# V2.1 Phase 3: listing price vs merchant-verified total

Scope: research (`research_purchase`), comparison (`compare_purchase_offers`) and the research-to-quote hand-over.
Mandate, capture and payment-provider code were not changed.

## 1. Terminology audit

Each place that names a price, classified by the stage that produced it.

| Place | What it says | Stage / basis | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| `intent.objective = "LOWEST_VERIFIED_TOTAL"` (ResearchIntentV2, PurchaseIntentRouter) | the user wants the cheapest | the user's **goal**, not a research result | Kept: it is a public contract value. Policy P3 states that research only approximates it with listing prices and that only compare meets it. |
| `decision.ranking[].price` `{ lowestListingMinor, confidence: "LISTING_PRICE_ONLY" }` | lowest eligible listing | search-time listing, item only | Correct. |
| `recommendation.whySelected` "Lowest eligible listing $X at m" | listing | listing | Correct. |
| `recommendation.priceEvidence.note` | "Search-time listing price … never a verified total." | listing | Correct. |
| Decision `DISCLAIMER` | "Listing prices are search-time prices, not verified totals." | listing | Correct. |
| Disqualifier `OVER_BUDGET` (research) | "lowest eligible listing … is over the budget" | listing vs budget | Correct and conservative: an item listing over budget cannot become a total under budget without a discount, and the user can research again. Gated as a hard item. |
| Blocker `BUDGET_TIGHT` | "within 10% of the budget while shipping and tax are unknown" | listing | Correct; resolved only by a merchant quote (verifier enforces the budget on the total). |
| Planner order for the cheapest objective (R-09) | listing price first | listing, spending order only | Correct: it orders verification spend, not a claim. |
| `comparison.lowestVerifiedEligible` | lowest verified eligible offer | merchant quote; any unknown component makes the total unknown and the offer ineligible | Correct. |
| `comparison.disclosure` | "Listing prices alone are never ranked as totals." | quote | Correct. |
| `publicOffer.listingPriceMinor` next to `cost` | both present | listing and quote | Was ambiguous for a reader: both numbers appear on one offer with no statement of which is which. **Fixed**: `priceProvenance` labels them. |
| Eval metric `falseVerifiedCheapest` | cheapest claim checked against world truth | quote | Correct (0/4 at both caps). |

No research-stage text presents a listing price as a total. The gap was at the hand-over: compare returned the quote and
the listing side by side without saying which one research had used, whether the quote moved, or that other research
candidates were never quoted.

## 2. Promotion policy (src/engine/QuotePromotion.ts)

* **P1.** A verified total exists only for an offer that MerchantOfferVerifier made eligible: a fresh merchant quote for
  that offer, the locked identity, the session currency, and every component known.
* **P2.** Promotion copies nothing from research. Verified figures come only from the quote; the research listing stays
  next to it, labelled `SEARCH_TIME_LISTING_PRICE`.
* **P3.** `LOWEST_VERIFIED_TOTAL` is the user's goal. Research approximates it with listing prices; it is met only by a
  compare result. When the objective is the cheapest and more than one candidate was eligible, compare reports
  `OTHER_CANDIDATES_NOT_QUOTED`: a lower verified total elsewhere is not excluded.
* **P4.** A quoted item price more than 5% away from the research listing is `PRICE_CHANGED_SINCE_RESEARCH`. If it went
  up and another eligible candidate had a lower research listing, `RESEARCH_ORDER_MAY_HAVE_CHANGED`.

`compare_purchase_offers` now returns `priceProvenance { status, research, verified, notes, explanation }` for V2
sessions. It is descriptive: eligibility, budget and stock are still enforced by the verifier on the quote, and payment
preparation by the PurchaseGate (Phase 1). The output contract only gains a field.

## 3. Not done (deliberate)

* Quoting every eligible research candidate to establish the true cheapest total: more provider and merchant calls per
  request; it needs a cost decision and is out of scope for an offline sprint.
* Renaming `LOWEST_VERIFIED_TOTAL`: it is an existing public value. The meaning is documented instead.
